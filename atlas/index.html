        Where both agree CDC and HeadWater

          CDC PLACES values are model based estimates for small areas, so in a small county a rank is a signal, not a measurement. HeadWater lists appear only for counties past the respondent floor and after the governance board approves release. "Where both agree" pairs each survey item with its closest CDC measure and lists the pairs where the county is in the top quarter of the state on the CDC measure and the item is in the county's top third of flagged answers.

          Respondent floor. A county shows HeadWater results only when it has at least 10 submitted surveys. Below that it is hatched, and its responses are held back until the floor is reached. Individual answers are never shown.

          Scores. The three HeadWater scales average their items on a 0 to 100 scale pointed in the stress direction, so a higher Financial Stress, Stress Load or Social Support score always means more strain. Item layers show the share of respondents giving the flagged answer, and the county panel shows the mean where the item has one.

          Public layer. CDC PLACES county estimates, age-adjusted percent of adults, from 2023 BRFSS (short sleep from 2022). Model-based estimates, not direct counts. Source: data.cdc.gov PLACES county data.
