How to use this site

This site measures chronic stress in Nebraska, county by county. It shows what the research says drives it and what helps. It is being built to point you to help that is close to home.

If you are here for yourself

  1. Take the survey. HeadWater takes 12 to 15 minutes. It is anonymous. Start the survey.
  2. See your county. Open the Atlas and tap your county. No survey needed.
  3. Find help near you. This part, the Resource Engine, is being built. For now, call 211 for local services.

Plain language results by area, shown when you finish the survey, are being built now.

If you are here for your community

  1. See your county on the Atlas.
  2. Read what drives it. Each concern links to its sources in the Research Library.
  3. See what works. Actions with graded evidence. This shelf is a partner preview while it is under review.
  4. Compare with what is in place, and find funding. Partner access. You can ask for it from the Atlas.

What people use it for

  • A grant writer copies a citation for a county's top concern.
  • A hospital starts its community health needs assessment from the county view.
  • A clinic hands out a QR code so patients can take the survey.
  • A county board member sees how the county compares on sleep, smoking or mental distress.

What it is not

It is not a diagnosis and not a medical device. It is not an emergency service. If you need to talk to someone now, call or text 988, or call 911. The survey is early. Reliability is measured for each subscale separately and reported that way.

Your privacy

The survey is anonymous. Nobody sees a name with an answer.

We never sell a person's answers. Person level data is released to no one.

Organizations see combined, de-identified county results only, and only what the data governance board approves.
